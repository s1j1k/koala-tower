🐨 Koala Tower: free ATC selection test practice

A gamified practice app for people applying to become air traffic controllers. Fly a little plane between lesson islands and train every skill the selection tests look for. Free, no sign-up, works on your phone.

✈️ Start practising

What the selection tests cover

Airservices Australia's online selection stage uses the SureSelect test battery (by Airways). Based on the public descriptions, it has three parts:

  • ATC Skill Simulations (about 1.5 hours, game-like): short-term memory, spatial awareness, remembering relationships, and a radar simulation that scores safety, efficiency and multi-tasking.
  • ATC Cognitive Ability Suite (about 45 minutes): detail accuracy and coded reasoning.
  • Behavioural Traits: a personality questionnaire. Answer it honestly; there's nothing to drill.

None of it needs aviation knowledge. What helps is being quick, accurate and calm under time pressure, and that improves with practice.

What you can practise

  • 🧠 Memory Mountains: digit span (forwards and backwards), flight strip recall, n-back, blip-grid position memory, and remembering relationships (links, chains and who is above whom).
  • 🧭 Spatial Sands: heading turns, clock-code traffic positions, bearings on rotated maps, mental rotation, path tracking and egocentric turns.
  • 📡 Radar Reef: conflict spotting from radar snapshots, a live sector-control simulation (separation and exit gates), and a multi-tasking drill.
  • 🔍 Detail Docks: same-or-different code checks, spot-the-difference tables and scan-and-count grids with look-alike characters.
  • 🔐 Code Canyon: code books, rule followers, symbol operations and code breaking.
  • 🧩 Puzzle Peaks: number series, number machines, missing operators, symbol sums and odd-one-out.
  • 🧮 Maths Meadows: mental arithmetic, unit conversions, speed–distance–time, estimation and percentages.

How it works

  • Every lesson has 10 levels. Earn a star to unlock the next level.
  • The daily flight picks five short lessons from your weakest areas.
  • The checkride runs a mock session in the same order as the real test, at practice, realistic or tough difficulty.
  • In the map view you steer by heading; in the side view you climb and descend through flight-level gates. Both are practice too.

Privacy

No accounts, no ads, no cookies. Your progress is saved only in your own browser on your own device. We count visits anonymously with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which doesn't use cookies or track you across sites. Nothing you type into the app leaves your device.

Not official

Koala Tower is an independent practice tool. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Airservices Australia, Airways or any test provider. All practice tasks are original and only resemble the style of the real tests. Good luck, future controller!
