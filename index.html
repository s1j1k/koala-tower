Quick rules to memorise
  • Per-minute speed: knots ÷ 60 = NM per minute. 240 kt = 4 NM/min. 300 kt = 5 NM/min.
  • Time to go: distance ÷ (NM per min). 60 NM at 240 kt = 60 ÷ 4 = 15 min.
  • Metres → feet: × 3.3 (exactly 3.28). 1,000 m ≈ 3,300 ft.
  • Feet → metres: × 0.3. 10,000 ft ≈ 3,000 m.
  • NM → km: × 1.85 (about double, minus 7.5%). 100 NM ≈ 185 km.
  • Knots → km/h: same as NM → km: × 1.85.
  • Flight levels: FL × 100 = feet. FL350 = 35,000 ft.
  • Reciprocal heading: under 180, add 180; otherwise subtract 180. Shortcut: +200 then −20 (070 → 250).
  • Closing speed: head-on, add the speeds. Same direction, subtract them.
  • Multiplying: split it up. 7 × 48 = 7 × 50 − 7 × 2 = 350 − 14 = 336.
